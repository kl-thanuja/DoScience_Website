We believe learning begins with curiosity.

When people have the opportunity to stop, interact, ask questions and discover something for themselves, ideas become experiences. And experiences stay with us.

We try to create those opportunities in simple, welcoming ways, through places, products and experiences that invite people to explore rather than simply be told.

Over the years, this idea has taken different forms.

DoScience, a science centre where children can experience ideas hands-on.

Story Box, a self-service dispenser of short content that brings small encounters with reading and learning into everyday spaces.

Science installations, rolling-ball sculptures and wind sculptures that make ideas tangible through interaction and movement.

Different forms, but the same intention:

to give people opportunities to stop, explore and discover.

Who we are

DoScience is an initiative of Bookmark Trust, a non-profit organisation working in the area of non-formal learning and public engagement.

Library Solutions, a social enterprise, develops and takes educational products into schools and other spaces.

The two organisations share a common belief in making learning more accessible, engaging and welcoming.

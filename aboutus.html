We believe that learning begins

with curiosity.

When people have the opportunity to stop, interact, ask questions and discover something for

themselves, ideas become experiences. And experiences stay with us.

We try to create those opportunities in simple, welcoming ways, through places, products and

experiences that invite people to explore rather than simply be told.

Over the years, this idea has

taken different forms.

DoScience, a science centre where learners can experience ideas hands-on.

Story Box, a self-service dispenser of short content that brings small encounters with reading and

learning into everyday spaces.

Science installations, rolling-ball sculptures and wind sculptures that make ideas tangible through

interaction and movement.

Different forms, but the same intention: to create opportunities to stop, explore and discover.

Who we are

DoScience is an initiative of Bookmark Trust, a non-profit organisation working in the area
of non-formal learning and public engagement.

Library Solutions, a social enterprise, develops and takes educational products

into schools and other spaces.

The two organisations share a common belief of making learning

more accessible, engaging and welcoming.

Our founders have years of corporate and social sector experience.

Aparna Vishwanatham provides strategic direction and leads day-to-day operations.

Akhil Vishwanatham provides guidance and mentorship to the team.

Our team comprises a diverse group of creative minds who enjoy building, learning and teaching.
We enjoy our work, and hope that you will too. The team is supported by advisors and mentors,
with longstanding experience in science, teaching and engineering.

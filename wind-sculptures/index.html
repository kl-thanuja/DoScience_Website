KINETIC WIND SCULPTURES

Wind, turned into movement.

We design and build kinetic wind sculptures that bring movement, form and curiosity into open spaces.

Wind sculptures change with the breeze, the light and the time of day. Their movement gives people something to notice, follow and return to - creating moments of curiosity, conversation and quiet observation.

And sometimes, simply watching something move brings a sense of calm.

For schools • campuses • parks
traffic islands • public spaces

A RANGE OF SCULPTURES

A range of forms.
A range of movement.

From simple rotating elements to complex combinations, our wind sculptures respond to the breeze in many ways. Explore the different kinds of movement they create.

Horizontal Axis

Fan-like forms that rotate around a horizontal axis, creating a strong sense of continuous movement.

Vertical Axis

Tree-like and other upright forms where elements rotate around a vertical axis, responding gently to the wind.

Multiple Axes & Combinations

Sculptures that bring together different moving elements or axes, creating more complex and varied patterns of movement.

Sound & Musical Forms

Wind-driven sculptures that combine movement with sound, adding another sensory dimension to the experience.

Built for open spaces.

Each sculpture is designed for outdoor environments, where wind, people and nature come together.

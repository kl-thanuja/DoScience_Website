Kinetic machines that turn motion, cause and effect into something you can follow.

A ball starts moving. One action sets off another, and another – creating a chain of movement that is fascinating to watch and easy to follow.

Hidden within this movement is a range of ideas from classical mechanics – motion, force, energy, momentum and gravity.

Our rolling ball sculptures invite people to stop, watch and discover the science at work in every movement.

Complex in movement.
Robust in construction.

There is a lot happening inside a rolling ball sculpture. We build our machines to be complex and fascinating to watch, while remaining stable, reliable and robust in everyday use.

The forms are designed to be visually engaging as well as mechanically sound. Our sculptures are made using mild steel, electroplated and finished with paint. They are non–electrical, require little maintenance, and can be used both indoors and outdoors.

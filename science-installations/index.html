Science you can see, touch and explore.

We design and build hands – on science installations that complement classroom learning and bring science concepts into everyday spaces across a school. They are practical to operate, robust, and designed to fit different spaces without requiring a large budget or a large space.

The installations give students an opportunity to see, touch and explore concepts for themselves. Teachers can use the models to support their lessons and help students make connections and discover how the underlying concepts work.

For schools • science centres • campuses • public spaces

A range of hands – on science.

Our installations cover Mechanics, Sound, Light and Mathematics, with concepts that connect to the school curriculum. Students can explore these ideas through physical interaction, observation and experimentation.

Mechanics

Explore concepts such as levers, pulleys, gears, momentum and rotational motion through models including Newton's Cradle, Giant Lever, Gyro Chair, Belt & Pulley Drive and Race the Rollers.

Sound

Explore how sound is produced, travels and behaves through models including Echo Tube, Hexagonal Chimes, Metal Gongs, Resonance Pipes and Delayed Sound.

Light

Explore reflection, image formation and visual perception through models including Multiple Reflections, Periscope, Giant Magic Mirror, Infinity Well and Antigravity Mirror.

Mathematics

Explore mathematical ideas through models including Plinko, Möbius Strip, Pythagoras Theorem, Value of Pi and Abacus.

Simple, easy to understand signages.

Each installation is supported by simple, easy to understand explanatory signages that show students how to interact with the model, explain the principle behind it, and connect the idea to everyday applications.

Designed for schools.
Built to last.

For a science installation to become part of a school, it needs to be useful in teaching, easy for students to use, and robust enough for everyday use. Our experience of running DoScience has helped us understand what works in practice.

We design and build models that teachers can use to support classroom teaching, and that students can return to, experiment with and explore in their own way.

Built using mild steel, aluminium and stainless steel, our installations are robust, designed to withstand rigorous use and require little maintenance. They are made to remain a useful part of the school environment for many years.

A model can become part of a physics lab, corridor, library or common space – a simple way to add hands–on science to everyday school life.

Science is public art.

Our science installations are also public art – bringing movement, interaction and curiosity into the places people pass through every day.

We design and build large installations for parks, traffic islands, street corners and other public spaces. These are visually engaging in their own right, while also giving people an opportunity to stop, interact and discover a science concept.

A well–placed installation does more than occupy a public space. It creates a small moment of curiosity and learning, becoming part of the social infrastructure of a neighbourhood or city.
